# OctoAcme Team Member Onboarding Checklist

## Purpose
This checklist helps new team members get up to speed quickly with OctoAcme's project management processes, tools, and team collaboration standards.

---

## Week 1: Foundation & Access

### Documentation Review
- [ ] Read [Project Management Overview](./octoacme-project-management-overview.md)
- [ ] Review [Roles and Personas](./octoacme-roles-and-personas.md) to understand team structure
- [ ] Familiarize yourself with [Project Initiation Guide](./octoacme-project-initiation.md)
- [ ] Review [Execution & Tracking](./octoacme-execution-and-tracking.md)

### System Access
- [ ] Obtain access to project management tools (e.g., GitHub Projects, Jira, etc.)
- [ ] Join team communication channels (Slack, Teams, etc.)
- [ ] Set up development environment (if applicable)
- [ ] Access documentation repositories and knowledge bases

### Team Introductions
- [ ] Meet with your manager for role expectations discussion
- [ ] Schedule 1:1 introductions with immediate team members
- [ ] Attend team standup or weekly sync meeting
- [ ] Review current sprint/iteration goals

---

## Week 2: Process & Workflow

### Role-Specific Orientation

#### For Developers
- [ ] Review coding standards and contribution guidelines
- [ ] Understand branching strategy and PR process
- [ ] Set up local development environment
- [ ] Complete first starter task or bug fix

#### For Business Analysts
- [ ] Review requirements documentation templates
- [ ] Understand stakeholder engagement process
- [ ] Shadow a requirements gathering session
- [ ] Review current project charters

#### For UX Designers
- [ ] Review design system and component libraries
- [ ] Access design tools (Figma, Sketch, etc.)
- [ ] Understand design review and approval process
- [ ] Review accessibility standards and guidelines

#### For QA Engineers
- [ ] Review test plan templates and testing frameworks
- [ ] Understand defect tracking and triage process
- [ ] Access test environments and automation tools
- [ ] Review quality metrics and standards

#### For Project Managers
- [ ] Review project charter templates and examples
- [ ] Understand risk management and communication protocols
- [ ] Access project tracking tools and dashboards
- [ ] Review stakeholder management approach

#### For Release Managers
- [ ] Review release process and deployment procedures
- [ ] Understand rollback and incident response protocols
- [ ] Access deployment tools and monitoring systems
- [ ] Review release checklists and runbooks

#### For Product Managers
- [ ] Review product roadmap and prioritization framework
- [ ] Understand user research and feedback processes
- [ ] Access analytics and metrics dashboards
- [ ] Review competitive analysis and market research

#### For Support Leads
- [ ] Access support ticketing system
- [ ] Review escalation procedures and SLAs
- [ ] Understand incident response protocols
- [ ] Review customer communication templates

### Cross-Functional Understanding
- [ ] Review [Cross-Role Collaboration section](./octoacme-roles-and-personas.md#cross-role-collaboration-and-handoffs)
- [ ] Understand handoff processes relevant to your role
- [ ] Identify key stakeholders you'll work with regularly

---

## Week 3-4: Active Participation

### Project Engagement
- [ ] Participate in sprint planning or iteration planning
- [ ] Contribute to ongoing project deliverables
- [ ] Attend relevant ceremonies (standups, reviews, retrospectives)
- [ ] Begin using [Role Handoff Checklist](./role-handoff-checklist.md) for collaborations

### Knowledge Sharing
- [ ] Share initial observations and questions with team
- [ ] Document any process improvements or clarifications needed
- [ ] Attend a retrospective and contribute feedback
- [ ] Review [Retrospective & Continuous Improvement](./octoacme-retrospective-and-continuous-improvement.md)

### Stakeholder Connections
- [ ] Meet with cross-functional partners relevant to your role
- [ ] Understand communication cadences and reporting requirements
- [ ] Participate in weekly syncs or status updates

---

## 30-Day Check-in

### Self-Assessment
- [ ] Can you explain OctoAcme's project management approach?
- [ ] Do you understand your role and responsibilities?
- [ ] Have you identified who to ask for help in different scenarios?
- [ ] Are you comfortable with the tools and systems you need?

### Manager Review
- [ ] Schedule 30-day check-in with your manager
- [ ] Discuss progress, challenges, and next learning goals
- [ ] Identify any gaps in knowledge or access
- [ ] Set goals for the next 30-60 days

---

## Continuous Learning

### Ongoing Development
- [ ] Participate in retrospectives and share improvement ideas
- [ ] Stay current with process documentation updates
- [ ] Contribute to knowledge base and documentation
- [ ] Mentor new team members as they onboard
